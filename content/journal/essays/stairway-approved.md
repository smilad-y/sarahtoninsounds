The new *Narnia: The Magician’s Nephew* [trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gDJ-ini7fF0) just dropped, and somehow, Greta Gerwig managed to get Led Zeppelin’s “Stairway to Heaven” cleared for it. Reportedly the first time the band has ever said yes to it on screen. EVER.

For anyone who doesn’t know, getting Led Zeppelin to approve a song for a film is notoriously difficult. They’re famously selective about licensing their music, and “Stairway to Heaven” has been especially hard to get. *Wayne’s World* even made a joke about it with the now-iconic “No Stairway? Denied!” scene, which is extra funny when you know that the original version of the movie actually had to swap out the opening notes of the song in later releases because of licensing issues. *Almost Famous* also famously ran into trouble trying to use the song. So the fact that Gerwig personally got Jimmy Page and Robert Plant on board for the *Narnia* trailer is actually history.

Getting music to picture is never as simple as picking the song. Some songs don’t really have a price. Sometimes it’s an artist’s ethical or moral reasons, and no amount of negotiating changes that. 

Anyway, I showed the trailer to my sister and brother-in-law, and afterward, he queued up “[Lazy Sunday](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sRhTeaa_B98)” by The Lonely Island. If you were around for the mid-2000s *SNL* era, you know the one. And as we were listening to it, I had a thought: **how funny would it be if they made a *Narnia* promo using “Lazy Sunday”?**

And then I started thinking about how funny it would be to get Andy Samberg and Chris Parnell to reprise their “Lazy Sunday” roles for a *Narnia* promo. Or have the cast lip-sync to the song. Or someone could take the track and turn it into an absurdly epic trailer edit, complete with dramatic cuts and sweeping shots of Narnia. I don’t know! I’m spitballing here, but the lyrics already reference *The Chronicles of Narnia*, and I think there’s something really fun to play with.

Imagine getting the millennials who grew up loving The Lonely Island to lose their minds over an unexpected “Lazy Sunday” revival while promoting a *Narnia* movie. It wouldn’t replace the epic “Stairway to Heaven” trailer or anything. That moment deserves its flowers. This would just be a fun little bonus that gets people talking.

I love thinking about these kinds of possibilities, especially with my music supervision background. So, just putting this idea out into the universe: what if Netflix did something fun with “Lazy Sunday” to promote *Narnia*? A little Lonely Island nostalgia, a little *Narnia*, and a very unexpected crossover. I think it could be hilarious.

Netflix, if you’re reading this, consider it a very enthusiastic and entirely unsolicited pitch!
