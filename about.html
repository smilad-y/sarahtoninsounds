Hi! I’m Sarah,
the girl behind Sarahtonin Sounds.

Music has been a constant in my life for as long as I can remember. Being the youngest of three meant I was always absorbing whatever was playing around me, from my sisters’ pop punk and Jesse McCartney phases to the Arabic music and old standards my parents loved. Then came LimeWire, burned CDs, and trips to Tower Records, where I bought my first CD, AFI’s Decemberunderground, when I was around ten. Growing up near San Pedro and around the South Bay’s punk history only added to it. I never really developed one musical lane. If something caught my ear, I was into it.

For a long time, I didn’t realize music could actually be my job. I got into law school right as the pandemic hit, and suddenly the plan I’d assumed I was following didn’t feel right anymore. Music was the thing I kept coming back to. Once I realized I could actually build a career around it, music supervision was what I wanted to pursue. It brought together everything I already loved about music: discovery, research, curation, and finding the right song for the right moment.

A few early opportunities eventually led me to TMZ Productions, where I got to do exactly that alongside my boss and mentor, Tony. Somewhere along the way, I stopped wondering whether skipping law school had been the wrong decision.

But Sarahtonin Sounds had been there before any of it.
I started it as my own little curation project before I ever worked professionally in music. Sarahtonin was a dumb little Sarah + serotonin joke that somehow became the perfect name. Music has always been one of the things that makes me happiest, and sharing it with people makes it even better. Apparently I couldn’t resist a pun.

Then I got distracted by work, and Sarahtonin Sounds got put on the shelf. After being laid off, bringing it back and actually giving it room to grow felt like the natural move. I don’t know exactly what it’ll turn into yet, and I kind of like that. I just know I want a place to share the playlists, discoveries, writing, and music I can’t stop talking about.

My taste is all over the place. Being Egyptian and growing up with Arabic music is a big part of my musical foundation, but really, if something moves me, I’m into it. I love finding hidden gems, whether it’s an artist about to break or a song from sixty years ago that’s criminally slept on. Music sparks my creativity, makes me invent entire scenes in my head, and gives me something to share with other people. That’s a huge part of why I love it.

Let’s Work Together!

I work across music supervision, licensing, curation, and music discovery, and I love collaborating with people on a good idea. If you think I could be a fit for something you’re working on, I’d love to hear about it.
